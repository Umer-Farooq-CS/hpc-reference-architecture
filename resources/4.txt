### **Requests for Proposals (RFPs) & Hardware Sizing**

* When evaluating RFPs, a core task is determining hardware sizing and resource division.
* An example scenario involves evaluating if the hardware is sufficient for 110 tenants utilizing 64 GPUs and 1 PB of total storage.
* A key operational consideration is that clients might not provide explicit requirements for how to divide these resources.
* The architect must determine if the hardware is enough and define the strategy for how to divide it.

### **Storage Architecture Breakdown**

Large-scale storage allocations (such as the 1 PB example) are divided into specific functional tiers:

* **Hot Storage:** Specifically allocated for RAG Vector data.
* **Cold Storage:** Used for Object Knowledge Bases, utilizing S3 or NFS.
* **Block Storage:** Dedicated to infrastructure components, including VMs, Kubernetes (K8s), Pods, and ETCD.

### **Reference Architecture (RA)**

* The team is responsible for providing the Reference Architecture (RA) for the proposed solutions.
* This architecture incorporates RDMA and aligns with Nvidia Architecture ("Nvidia Arch").
* All designs must be executed "According to Standards".
* **Rafay Integration Structure:** The control plane consists of a "Rafay Controller" that interfaces downwards to "Rafay Agents". These agents are deployed directly onto the "Bare Metal / Cloud" infrastructure layer.
